This Agreement is made on Tuesday, 22/03/2022, between:
First Party: Modern Build Co., located in Riyadh, represented by Mary Rahman, Project Manager.
Second Party: BrightPath Technologies Inc., represented by David Khoury, Chief Financial Officer.
Whereas the First Party wishes to engage the Second Party for the supply of food products to the cafeteria, the parties have agreed as follows:
1. If the Second Party fails to deliver on time, the First Party may deduct a penalty of 0.5% for each week of delay.
2. The Second Party shall perform the agreed works in accordance with the technical specifications attached hereto.
3. This Agreement shall be valid for 24 months from the date of signature and may be renewed by written consent of both parties.
4. The First Party shall pay the Second Party a total amount of 211,000.00 AED in 6 equal instalments.
5. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of Egypt, and the courts of Cairo shall have jurisdiction over any dispute arising from it.
